Running case

Chattanooga Precision Components (CPC) is a mid-cap, single-plant Tennessee manufacturer that sells 30 percent of its output to a German distributor in euros. It owns a small Mexican warehousing subsidiary and competes against a Japanese rival in its home market. CPC appears in every chapter.
